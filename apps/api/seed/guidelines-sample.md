# Arocall Call Handling Guidelines (sample)

Company rules for how calls must be handled. Arocall uses this document as the
source of truth when scoring calls with AI.

## Greeting
- Greet the customer within the first 30 seconds of the call.
- State your name and the company, and offer help.
- Example: "Hello, thank you for calling Arocall, my name is Ana, how can I help you today?"

## Identification and compliance
- Verify the caller's identity before sharing account details.
- Never promise refunds, timelines, or outcomes you cannot guarantee.
- Follow the recording-notice and consent policy on every call.

## Tone and empathy
- Acknowledge feelings explicitly (e.g. "I understand how frustrating this is, I hear you, and I am sorry for the trouble").
- Do not interrupt. Let the customer finish before responding.

## Do's
- Summarize what was agreed before closing.
- State a clear next step with a timeline (e.g. "I will fix this by tomorrow").

## Don'ts
- Don't blame the customer or other teams.
- Don't invent policy details — say you will check and follow up.

## Escalation
- Offer a supervisor or a callback when the issue cannot be resolved in one call.
- Flag harassment, health, or safety concerns to a human evaluator.

## Closing
- Ask "Is there anything else I can help with today?"
- Thank the customer and close politely (e.g. "Thank you so much, goodbye!").
